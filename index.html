Safari on iPhone can't hide its bars for a web page, but Cheese Toast runs full screen when you start it from your Home Screen:

  1. Tap the … button.
  2. Choose Share.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Cheese Toast from its new Home Screen icon.
